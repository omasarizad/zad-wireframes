Can You Read the Quran during Your Period?
Sheikh Muhammed Salih Al-Munajjid

Praise be to Allah. This is an important question that concerns many Muslim women. The scholars have differed on this issue...

The Majority View (Prohibition)

Most classical jurists hold that women cannot recite Quran during menstruation until they achieve ritual purity. Limited exceptions exist for brief phrases like "Bismillahi'r-Rahmani'r-Rahim"...

The Minority View (Permissibility)

Some scholars, including Malik and Ibn Taymiyah, argue recitation is allowed, reasoning that no clear evidence explicitly forbids it and that preventing recitation deprives women of spiritual reward.

While reciting from memory may be permissible under some interpretations, it is forbidden to touch the mus-haf when one is in any kind of state of impurity.

And Allah knows best.

Recommended Implementation Order
1
Answer Page (Desktop) — Top banner + post-answer CTA + sticky sidebar
Balanced→

Reading flow fully preserved — no mid-content interruption. Top banner captures first impression, post-answer CTA leverages reciprocity, sidebar provides sustained visibility.

2
Search Page (Desktop) — Single inline after 3rd result + sidebar
Balanced→

Users see 3 real results before any ad — search stays useful. No top banner keeps search bar prominent.

3
Homepage (Desktop) — Top banner + sidebar
Balanced→

Homepage is navigational — users tolerate ads here more. Two zones without interrupting content sections.

4
Answer Page (Mobile) — Post-answer + slim dismissible sticky
Balanced→

Slim 50px sticky minimizes viewport use. Dismissible respects user choice. Post-answer CTA at natural stopping point.

5
Contextual CTAs — Popover, post-download, post-question
Contextual→

Non-blocking, high-engagement triggers. Post-download and post-question moments have strongest reciprocity — user just received value.

6
Search + Homepage (Mobile) — Top banner or single inline
Balanced→

Mobile users navigate quickly — single ad per page avoids scroll fatigue. Completes full cross-page coverage.

Implementation Spec Sheet
PropertyValueNotes
Dismiss behaviorsessionStorageKey: dismissed-{zoneId}. Non-redisplay within same session. Resets on new session/tab.
RTL supportFull mirroringSidebar flips to right. Ad dismiss button mirrors. CTA alignment mirrors. All text direction via dir="rtl".
AccessibilityWCAG 2.1 AAAd zones: role="complementary" + aria-label. Dismiss button: visible focus ring, aria-label="Dismiss ad". Contrast ratios meet 4.5:1 minimum.
Native cardFluid widthInherits parent container width. Purple border #a78bfa, bg #f5f3ff. Title #5b21b6 (7.2:1 contrast). No "Sponsored" label.
Sticky bottom (mobile)320 × 50Max 10% viewport. Dismiss persists via sessionStorage. Does not overlay content scroll.
Question submissionNever obstructedNo ad covers "Send a question" link, search bar, or form submit buttons.
Book/fatwa downloadNever obstructedDownload/print actions remain accessible. Post-download CTA appears after action completes.
